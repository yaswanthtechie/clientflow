# Product Requirements Document

## Product summary

ClientFlow helps an independent freelancer manage clients, projects, tasks, billable time, invoices, and outstanding payments from one application.

## Problem

Freelancers often track work, hours, and invoices in separate spreadsheets or applications. This makes billing slower and makes it difficult to see what is unpaid.

## Primary user

An independent freelancer with multiple clients and projects.

## MVP goal

A user can register, create a client and project, add a task and time entry, create an invoice from unbilled time, and see invoice status on a dashboard.

## In scope

- JWT authentication
- Client, project, task, and time-entry management
- Invoice generation and status tracking
- Dashboard reporting
- PDF invoice and CSV export

## Out of scope

- Payment processing and bank integrations
- Multi-user teams and client portals
- Complex tax rules and multiple currencies
- Mobile application
